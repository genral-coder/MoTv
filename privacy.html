Privacy Policy

My Video Tool — Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account, the Application accesses only the data required to provide its features: your public profile information (name and username) and the ability to create and post videos to your own TikTok account.

2. TikTok OAuth

The Application uses TikTok's official OAuth flow. We never ask for or store your TikTok password. Access tokens are stored locally on your computer and are used only to publish content you explicitly request.

3. Local Storage

Your videos, projects, and settings are stored locally on your device. No video content is uploaded to any server controlled by us.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or as needed to provide platform services (e.g., TikTok API requests you initiate).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete any project or video from the Application at any time. You can revoke the Application's access to your TikTok account from your TikTok settings, which removes our ability to act on your behalf.

6. Third-Party Services

When you publish, TikTok may receive the content you post, and its own Privacy Policy applies to that data. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@myvideotool.app
