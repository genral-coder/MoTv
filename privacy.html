Privacy Policy

My Video Tool — Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Process

My Video Tool is a desktop application that publishes videos to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API. To provide this service, the application processes:

  • TikTok account data (display name, username, avatar, follower and engagement statistics) that you authorize us to access when you connect your TikTok account.
  • Video files that you explicitly select and choose to publish from your device. These files are uploaded to TikTok only when you click "Publish".
  • Captions and hashtags that you write or that the application generates for your posts.

2. How We Use Information

  • To identify you and show your account information inside the application.
  • To upload and publish the videos you choose, using the access token you granted us.
  • To show your account statistics (followers, views, likes) on the dashboard.
  • To improve the application's features and user experience.

3. Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your own device. We do not operate cloud servers that collect, store, or transmit your content outside of what is required to communicate with TikTok's official API endpoints.

4. Data We Share

We do not sell or rent your personal information. The only third party we communicate with is TikTok (via its official API) to perform actions you explicitly requested: fetching your profile data and publishing your videos.

5. Access Tokens

Access tokens granted through TikTok's OAuth flow are stored securely on your device and are used only to call TikTok's official APIs. You can revoke our access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

6. Security

We follow reasonable security practices to protect the data stored on your device. Since data remains on your machine, we recommend protecting your device with a password and keeping your TikTok credentials private.

7. Your Rights

You can delete the application and all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the application and removing its data folder. You can also disconnect your TikTok account at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
